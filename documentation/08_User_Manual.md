## 1. Getting Started with Sankalp

Welcome to **Sankalp**! This user manual guides you through every feature of the application, from initial launch to taking full-length All-India Mock Tests.

### Launching the App
When you open Sankalp:
1. You will see an **Animated Splash Screen** displaying the golden emblem of governance and the tagline *"Prepare Smarter. Serve Better."*
2. If this is your first time, you will be guided through a **3-Step Onboarding Walkthrough** introducing Live Lectures, Mock Tests with OMR, and Syllabus Tracking. Tap **Get Started** or **Skip** to reach the Login Screen.
3. On the **Login Screen**, you can enter your email and password, or simply tap **"Quick Demo Login"** to enter immediately with preloaded candidate data (`atharva@example.com`).

## 2. Navigating the 5-Tab Home Shell

Sankalp features an intuitive bottom navigation bar with 5 primary sections:

```
[ 🏠 Home ]  [ 📺 Live Classes ]  [ 📝 Test Series ]  [ 📰 Current Affairs ]  [ 👤 Profile ]
```

### Tab 1: Home Dashboard (`Home`)
- **Top Bar**: Shows your profile picture, exam target ("CSE 2027"), notification bell, and one-tap **Dark/Light Mode toggle**.
- **Search Bar**: Type any subject (e.g. *"Polity"*, *"CSAT"*, *"Ethics"*) to filter lectures, courses, and tests instantly.
- **Your Progress Hub**: Tracks your study streak (12 days 🔥), questions solved (1,248), tests attempted (18), and overall syllabus completion circle.
- **Quick Jump Grid**: Five 1-tap shortcuts:
  1. 📘 **Syllabus Tracker**: Jump straight to your subject progress.
  2. 📜 **PYQs (2020-2025)**: Access official previous year questions with answer keys.
  3. 🏆 **Topper Talks**: Watch masterclasses by AIR 1, 3, 8, and 14.
  4. 💬 **Community**: Join aspirant discussion threads.
  5. 📝 **Notes**: Create and manage your personal revision summaries.

### Tab 2: Live Classes (`Live Classes`)
- Browse **Live Now** (active with red badge) and **Upcoming Scheduled Lectures**.
- Tap **"Join Live"** to enter the simulated classroom:
  - **Live Chat**: Type and send messages to faculty and peers.
  - **Live Polls**: Participate in multiple-choice quizzes with real-time percentage graphs.
  - **Ask Doubt**: Post dedicated questions for educator review.

### Tab 3: All-India Test Series (`Test Series`)
- Select from **Full-Length Mock Tests**, **Sectional Tests**, or **CSAT Paper-II**.
- Tap any test card to review the syllabus, duration (120 mins), and marking rules (+2.0 / -0.66).
- Tap **"Start Test"** to open the full exam simulator:
  - Use the top right **OMR Grid icon** to open the question navigation drawer.
  - Mark questions as **Answered** (Green), **Marked for Review** (Orange), or **Visited** (Red).
  - Submit the test to view your **Scorecard**, **All-India Rank**, **Percentile**, and **Subject Diagnostic Charts**.

### Tab 4: Current Affairs & Editorials (`Current Affairs`)
- Read daily curated editorials mapped directly to GS Papers (GS-I to GS-IV).
- Inside any article:
  - Read **Prelims Pointers** and the **Mains Practice Question**.
  - Tap **"Add to My Notes"** to import key facts into your digital notebook.
  - Tap the top-right quiz banner to take the **Daily 5-Question Quiz**.

### Tab 5: Profile & Settings (`Profile`)
- Review your account details, target exam year, and optional subject.
- Access your **Order History & Invoices**, **Bookmarked Items**, and **Study Reminders**.
- Toggle system preferences including **Dark Mode** and notifications.

## 3. How to Use the Syllabus Completion Tracker

1. From the Home screen, tap **"Syllabus Tracker"**.
2. Select any subject (e.g., *Indian Polity & Governance*).
3. Tap on any topic (e.g., *Fundamental Rights: Articles 12 to 35*).
4. Tap the status chip to cycle through:
   - ⚪ **Not Started**
   - 🟡 **In Progress**
   - 🟢 **Completed**
5. Notice how your overall completion percentage on the Home dashboard updates automatically!

---

## 4. How to Create and Manage Study Notes

1. From the Quick Jump grid on Home, tap **"Notes"**.
2. Tap the floating **"+"** button at the bottom right.
3. Enter your note title, select a subject (e.g., *Modern Indian History*), specify a topic, and type your revision content.
4. Tap the **Checkmark (✔)** icon in the top right to save.
5. Your notes are stored permanently on your device and will be available even after closing the app!
